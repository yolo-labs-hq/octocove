# Decisions

## Licence and contribution provenance

**Licence.** Octocove's substrate, CLI, codebase and documentation are open source under Apache-2.0, with `Copyright 2026 YOLO Labs` (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`). Apache-2.0 allows permissive adoption and includes an explicit contributor patent grant. YOLO Studio is a separate, closed-source product and one integration of Octocove; none of its source is in this repository. Package `private: true` settings control npm publication only; they are not licence restrictions.

**Contribution provenance.** Contribution commits require a DCO 1.1 sign-off; there is no CLA. The sign-off certifies the contributor's right to submit the work and makes attribution explicit, including responsibility for AI-assisted work. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) states the sign-off and provenance requirements.

**Consequence.** Anyone may use, modify and redistribute Octocove under Apache-2.0, keeping its licence and notices. Because contributions are accepted under the DCO without a CLA, contributors keep their copyright, and relicensing the project would need the consent of its contributors; a CLA would be introduced only for a concrete relicensing or business need.
